Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Life Manager ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile productivity application. This policy explains what data we access, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data we access

When you connect a Google or Microsoft (Outlook) account, we request read-only access to your calendar events. Specifically:

  • Your calendar events (title, start time, end time, reminders)

We request the minimum permissions required to display your events. We never request permission to create, edit, or delete your events.

2. How we use your data

  • Calendar events are fetched directly from Google Calendar API and Microsoft Graph API and displayed within the app on your device
  • Access tokens are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage (Expo SecureStore)
  • No data is transmitted to our servers — we do not operate any backend

3. Data storage

All data (tokens, account labels, color preferences) is stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

4. Third parties

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party. The only external services we communicate with are:

  • Google Calendar API — to fetch your Google calendar events
  • Microsoft Graph API — to fetch your Outlook calendar events

5. Revoking access

You can disconnect any account at any time from within the app. You can also revoke access directly:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Microsoft: myapps.microsoft.com

6. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
